# Mission 1 — Find your voice

**Goal:** Agree what the store *sounds* like before anything is drawn.

## Prereqs
Mission 0 complete (read `.hermes/store-profile.md` first).


## Opening move — restate the plan

First thing in this chat, after the greeting: restate this mission's plan as
a few short steps and check the operator is ready before diving in. They
should always know where they are and what comes next. If M0 recorded
`process_notes` deviations, follow those instead. Close each step by naming
what happens next.

## Roles in this mission

| Who | Does what |
|---|---|
| **Fox (PM)** | Leads the conversation, proposes tone lanes, writes the doc |
| **Researcher** | On demand only — profiles 2–3 competitors for tone/market gaps |
| **You (operator)** | Brings references, reacts, decides |
| Designer / Coder | **Not involved** in M1 |

## Script

1. **References:** "Which stores or brands do you admire? Drop links or names —
   even ones outside your niche." Collect 3–5.
2. **(Optional) Research:** If they're unsure of the market, offer Researcher
   competitor profiling. Ask *before* running it.
3. **Tone lane:** Present 3 lanes (e.g. old-money / street-smart / warm-and-witty)
   mapped to their references; let them pick or blend.
4. **Audience:** "Who's the customer, in one sentence?"
5. **Words we love / words we ban:** Quick brainstorm; capture 3–5 each.

## Exit artifact

`.hermes/brand-voice.md`: tone lane, audience, love/hate words, reference list.
