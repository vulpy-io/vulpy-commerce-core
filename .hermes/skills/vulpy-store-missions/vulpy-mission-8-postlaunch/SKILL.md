# Mission 8 — After the grand opening

**Goal:** Show what Vulpy can do now that the store exists.

## Prereqs
Mission 7 complete. Read `store-profile.md` + `.hermes/launch-evidence.md`.


## Opening move — restate the plan

First thing in this chat, after the greeting: restate this mission's plan as
a few short steps and check the operator is ready before diving in. They
should always know where they are and what comes next. If M0 recorded
`process_notes` deviations, follow those instead. Close each step by naming
what happens next.

## Roles in this mission

| Who | Does what |
|---|---|
| **Fox (PM)** | The whole mission — pure conversation + tour |
| You (operator) | React, ask questions, pick a first adventure |
| Designer / Coder / etc. | Behind the scenes when the operator picks an adventure |

## Script

1. **The toolkit tour.** Explain (grouped, plain language):
   - **Design changes** — restyle sections, new pages, moodboard refreshes
   - **Marketing** — campaigns, newsletter, promotions, launch content
   - **Analytics & reports** — Matomo, orders, revenue, product performance
   - **External APIs / integrations** — connect services, automate workflows
   - **Content & blog** — posts, SEO, pages
2. **"This all continues in normal chats."** Make explicit: no mission structure
   needed anymore — "just ask me, anytime, in any chat."
3. **Suggest 2–3 sample first adventures**, e.g.:
   - "Want me to set up your newsletter?"
   - "Analyse your first 10 visitors?"
   - "Draft a launch-week promotion?"
4. **Mark the journey complete.** Recommend archiving remaining mission chats;
   the missions served their purpose.

## Exit artifact

`.hermes/journey-complete.md`: what was built, operator's chosen next adventure
(if any), capabilities tour acknowledged.

## Completion

"**The journey is complete.** You know what I can do — and I'm always one
message away. 🦊"
